# Product-page font assets

The page bundles its font assets locally so GitHub Pages, screenshots and offline previews do not depend on a Google Fonts network request.

- `Google Sans Flex` — display headings;
- `Google Sans Text` — body copy, labels and interface text;
- `Google Sans` — Latin product name and numeric readouts;
- `Noto Sans SC` — Chinese glyph coverage and variable weights.

These files are third-party font assets and are not relicensed by the repository's MIT license. Preserve their upstream terms when redistributing the product page; `Noto Sans SC` follows the SIL Open Font License, while the Google Sans files must remain under the terms of their original source asset.
